# Dashboard Page Override

## Purpose
The dashboard is the hotel manager/front-office command center, not a generic analytics page.

## KPI priority
1. Occupancy / rooms sold
2. Arrivals
3. Departures
4. In-house guests
5. Room/total revenue
6. ADR / RevPAR when live data is available

POS metrics remain secondary on the hotel dashboard.

## Interaction
Clickable KPIs should navigate to underlying operational lists once live wiring is available. Keep business date and operational context visible. Avoid charts that require hover to understand the value.
